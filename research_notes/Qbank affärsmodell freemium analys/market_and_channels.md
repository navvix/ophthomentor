Scope note: USA and Europe only. Figures carry the year of the underlying data. Items marked **[estimate]** or **[inference]** are mine, not from a source. ## Key question 1 — Market size: trainees in training (USA, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Nordics, Netherlands, Europe overall)

### Takeaway
The USA has ~1,760 ophthalmology residents in 126 ACGME programs with ~520–530 entering each year; the five large European countries alone add roughly 1,000 new trainees per year (Spain 224, Italy 245, France 151, Germany ~350 completions/yr, UK ~350 Part 1 sittings/yr), so the combined US+Europe trainee stock is plausibly 6,000–8,000 at any time **[estimate]**. The US cohort is the only one where every trainee sits the same high-stakes annual exam (OKAP) and most programs already pay for a qbank.

### Inferences
- **[estimate]** US trainee stock ≈ 1,800 residents (2025-26), with ~525 new entrants and ~525 graduates per year, given 1,764 residents in 2023-24, 528 match positions in 2026 and 531 third-years.
- **[estimate]** Annual new-trainee inflow in Europe's five largest markets ≈ 1,100–1,300: Spain 224 (2025), Italy 245 (2024), France 151 (2025), Germany ≈350 (using completions as a proxy for intake), UK ≈ 300–350 (using ~350 Part 1 sittings per October sitting and two sittings/yr with resits). With 5-year training (DE, ES 4 yrs, IT 4 yrs, FR 5–6 yrs, UK 7 yrs), the European trainee stock is plausibly 5,000–6,500 **[estimate]**.
- **[estimate]** Practising ophthalmologists in Europe's big five plus NL ≈ 28,000–30,000 (DE 8,254; IT ~8,900; FR 5,543; ES ≈4,700 at 9.9/100k × ~48m; NL 714; UK unknown but likely 1,500–2,000 consultants). Europe overall is likely 40,000–50,000, but this is not sourced.
- **[inference]** The EBO population (250–550 candidates/year) is a small fraction of European trainees; the EBO is mandatory or substitutes for the national exam only in Switzerland, France, Belgium, Austria and Ireland (see Q2), so EBO-focused prep is a niche within a niche. Falling pass rates (89.9% → 84.5% → 81.2% between 2023 and 2025) raise the stakes slightly.

## Key question 2 — Market size: practitioners and secondary segments (optometrists, orthoptists, ophthalmic nurses)

### Takeaway
There are roughly 21,000 FTE US ophthalmologists (2020) and 23,529 ABO diplomates (2024), of whom ~14,000 are in time-limited Continuing Certification and ~3,900 a year answer ABO Quarterly Questions; optometrists (47,800 US jobs, 2024) dwarf ophthalmology but need different content. In Europe, ESCRS alone attracts 8,000 doctors and 1,000 nurses/technicians to one congress.

### Cited Findings
- US workforce: an estimated 21,250 FTE ophthalmologists in 2020; supply projected to fall 12% (−2,650 FTE) and demand to rise 24% (+5,150 FTE) by 2035, i.e. 70% adequacy, second-worst of 38 specialties; 77% adequacy metro vs 29% non-metro — [AAO workforce projections report as filed with Minnesota Senate, 2025 (PDF)](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/archive/minutes/senate/2025/health/20250225/Health_20250225_SF1144-American-Academy-Of-Ophthamology-Ophthalmology-Workforce-Projections-In-US-Report.pdf); [Review of Ophthalmology](https://www.reviewofophthalmology.com/article/contemplating-the-workforce-deficit); [Healio, Jan 2024](https://www.healio.com/news/ophthalmology/20240103/practice-model-may-evolve-with-upcoming-ophthalmologist-shortage)
- ABO diplomates (2024): 23,529 total = 14,694 time-limited + 8,835 lifetime certificates; 14,111 time-limited diplomates participating in Continuing Certification as of 1 Jan 2024; 2023 recertification: 1,062 recertified, 93% retention — [ABO recertification statistics](https://abop.org/maintain-certification/recertification-statistics); [ABO 2023 scorecard (PDF)](https://abop.org:443/media/188208/2023_scorecard_final.pdf)
- ABO Quarterly Questions: 3,906 diplomates completed the 2023 assessment, 98.7% pass (passing standard 65%); 2022: 3,838 completed, 99.0% pass — [ABO recertification statistics](https://abop.org/maintain-certification/recertification-statistics)
- US optometrists: ~47,800 jobs in 2024; median wage $134,830 (May 2024); projected +8% employment 2024–34 — [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook: Optometrists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/optometrists.htm)
- UK orthoptists: BIOS membership "1,511+" (2024); ~1,100 practising orthoptists in the British Isles — [Wikipedia: BIOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_and_Irish_Orthoptic_Society); [Swansea Bay NHS orthoptics careers](https://sbuhb.nhs.wales/hospitals/a-z-services/orthoptics/orthoptics-folder/education-and-careers/)

- ESCRS 2025 Copenhagen: 15,342 participants = 8,005 doctors, 5,602 industry, 1,000 nurses/technicians, 403 healthcare professionals, 332 staff; 138 countries — [MCI: ESCRS 2025 case study](https://www.wearemci.com/en-ch/escrs-2025-a-world-class-congress-orchestrated-by-mci); figures repeated in the [ESCRS London 2026 Sales Manual (PDF)](https://congress.escrs.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ESCRS-London-2026-A4-Sales-Manual-5-May-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- **[inference]** The practising-ophthalmologist segment that actually has an exam-like trigger in the US is the ~14,000 time-limited diplomates in Continuing Certification, and in particular the ~3,900/year who sit Quarterly Questions; the 98–99% pass rate means stakes are very low, so paid prep demand will be weak compared with residents.
- **[inference]** Optometrists/orthoptists are numerically large (47,800 US optometrists vs ~1,800 residents) but take different exams (NBEO etc.), so they are a content-expansion option, not an adjacent audience for the same bank.

## Key question 3 — Exam calendar, structure, stakes, pass rates

### Takeaway
The US has a predictable twin peak: OKAP every mid-March (all residents, 260 items, $390/resident) and the ABO WQE every September (649 candidates in 2025, 85.1% first-attempt pass); the UK's FRCOphth Part 1 is the hardest high-stakes exam in the set (~50% pass, 352 candidates in Oct 2025) with Part 1/Part 2 sittings spread through Jan, Apr, Oct and Dec; the EBO is twice a year (May Paris / Oct online), €600, 80–90% pass; Germany's Facharztprüfung is an oral exam with no national MCQ.

### Inferences
- **[inference]** 2026 OKAP date: the AAO page lists only 2025 (Mar 13–16) and 2027–2029 (second week of March); 2026 was almost certainly mid-March 2026 (already past as of Oct 2026). Marketing peaks should be Dec–Mar (OKAP) and Jun–Sep (WQE) in the US.
- **[inference]** Stakes ranking for paid prep: FRCOphth Part 1 (≈50% pass, mandatory for progression, UK-only ~350/sitting) > ABO WQE (≈15% first-attempt fail, 649/yr) > OKAP (no pass/fail but percentile reported to program directors and predictive of WQE) > EBOD (80–90% pass, voluntary in most countries, €600) > ABO Quarterly Questions (99% pass). The German Facharztprüfung is an oral exam **[widely known; not directly verified in a fetched source]**, so MCQ prep demand there is for the FEBO or for knowledge, not for a national MCQ.
- **[inference]** The UK calendar gives four marketing windows per year (Jan, Apr, Oct Part 1; Dec Part 2 Written) versus two in the US; the EBO gives May and October.

## Key question 7 — Conversion triggers (exam proximity, cohort use, PD endorsement, analytics, free quotas)

### Takeaway
Direct conversion data for this niche does not exist publicly; what is documented is (a) the exam calendar itself, (b) a measured link between qbank use and OKAP percentile and between OKAP and WQE success, which makes program endorsement credible, and (c) the industry-standard free-quota pattern (OphthoQuestions 29 questions; AMBOSS 5 days/50 questions; Rosh free trial without card).

- Online qbank use correlates with OKAP percentile; program-arranged review sessions and OKAP-specific didactics also correlate; authors recommend incentives and qbank access — [BMC Medical Education 2019](https://link.springer.com/10.1186/s12909-019-1637-4)

- **[inference]** 29 free questions (OphthoQuestions) is the niche's reference free quota; a materially larger free tier (hundreds of questions) would be differentiated, and the peer-made free-resource culture (Anki, EyeGuru) suggests free users will share it.

## Key question 8 — Pricing norms and friction: VAT, currency, regional pricing

### Takeaway
EU/UK B2C digital sales must charge destination-country VAT (17% Luxembourg to 27% Hungary in the EU; One Stop Shop registration above a €10,000 EU-wide threshold), e-learning is generally at the standard rate, and the category leader (OphthoQuestions) prices in USD only with no regional variation — leaving a gap for EUR/GBP pricing that matches the UK's £99/6-month norm.

### Cited Findings
- EU VAT on electronically supplied services (SaaS, online courses, e-books, software) is charged at the customer's country rate under the destination principle, for EU and non-EU sellers alike; B2C cross-border sales use the One Stop Shop (OSS); the EU-wide distance-selling threshold is €10,000; standard rates range from 17% (Luxembourg) to 27% (Hungary) — [Kintsugi EU VAT guide 2026](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/europe/european-union.md); [Fungies: EU VAT digital services 2026](https://fungies.io/eu-vat-digital-services-merchant-of-record-2026/); [Avalara: global VAT/GST on digital services](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/vatlive/global-vat-gst-on-e-services.html?sa=X)
- E-books often qualify for reduced rates (5–9% in some jurisdictions) but SaaS and most software fall under standard rates — [Fungies: EU VAT digital products](https://fungies.io/eu-vat-digital-products)
- ESCRS and EBO quote prices "excl VAT" / in EUR; RCOphth and UK banks price in GBP (Eyedocs £99/6 months); OphthoQuestions prices in USD only with no regional pricing — [ESCRS 2026 Sales Manual](https://congress.escrs.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ESCRS-London-2026-A4-Sales-Manual-5-May-2026.pdf); [Eye News](https://www.eyenews.uk.com/education/training/post/choosing-the-right-frcopth-part-1-question-bank-my-experience-with-eye-academy-eyedocs-and-efrcophth); [OphthoQuestions pricing](https://www.ophthoquestions.com/help/pricing)
- Regional/PPP pricing claims from SaaS vendors' content (secondary, promotional sources — treat as indicative only): localized pricing said to raise revenue ~30% vs straight currency conversion; Atlassian reportedly saw +21% European conversion after country-specific pricing; 78% of SaaS companies above $100M ARR said to use regional pricing — [Monetizely](https://www.getmonetizely.com/articles/how-can-purchasing-power-parity-transform-your-global-saas-pricing-strategy); [Fungies PPP pricing 2026](https://fungies.io/ppp-pricing-saas-boost-global-revenue-2026)
- Purchasing-power context from this research: German new specialists ~346/yr with 8,254 practising (high-income market); Spain 9.9 ophthalmologists/100k with 224 MIR places; Italy 245 contracts; UK exam fees £330–£1,904 per part borne personally — see Q1/Q3 sources

### Inferences
- **[inference]** A US-headquartered seller must still register for OSS (or use a merchant of record) once EU B2C sales exceed €10,000/yr; with ~20–27% VAT on top, a $360 USD price becomes ≈ €400–420 gross in Germany, roughly 4× the UK norm for a Part 1 bank — a strong argument for separate EUR/GBP price lists.
- **[inference]** Competitors do not use regional pricing in this niche (OphthoQuestions USD-only; UK banks GBP-only and UK-only), so currency-native EUR/GBP pricing is itself a differentiator rather than a race to the bottom.
- **[inference]** Within Europe, Germany/Switzerland/Nordics can bear near-US prices; Spain/Italy/Portugal/Eastern Europe (where EBO candidates concentrate — Portugal, Greece, Poland, Romania appear in the EBO country list) likely need 30–50% lower price points.

### Gaps
- No survey evidence on European trainees' perception of USD pricing fairness.
- UK VAT treatment specifics for online exam prep (standard 20% is expected but not verified in a fetched source).
- No competitor in ophthalmology was found to publish PPP/regional pricing, so there is no benchmark of uplift in this niche.
